# Mahlangu Online Solutions — Portfolio Website

The personal portfolio and services website of **Musa Njabulo Mahlangu**, founder of **Mahlangu Online Solutions** in Pretoria, Gauteng.

The site presents career, brand and digital services for job seekers and small businesses. It covers the services, client results, the working process, pricing and testimonials. Visitors can send an enquiry straight to WhatsApp or email.

🔗 **Live site:** [portfolio-sand-five-b7fhxvcam9.vercel.app](https://portfolio-sand-five-b7fhxvcam9.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **One-page layout:** hero, about, services, results, process, education & skills, pricing, testimonials and contact
- **Eight service areas:** Career Services, Brand & Design, Professional Profiles, Websites & Digital, Research Support, Finance Tracking, Admin Support and AI Workflow Training
- **Pricing switcher:** toggles between once-off packages, finance retainers and AI training plans
- **Service detail sheets:** each service opens a panel with more information
- **WhatsApp & email enquiry form:** builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message or email. No data is stored on the site.
- **"Select package" shortcuts:** pre-fill the enquiry form with the chosen service
- **Responsive design:** works on phones, tablets and desktops
- **Fast and self-contained:** fonts and images are self-hosted, with no frameworks, no build step and no tracking

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5:** semantic, accessible markup
- **CSS3:** custom properties (design tokens), Flexbox and Grid
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** menu, pricing switcher, service sheets and the enquiry form
- **Fonts:** Poppins and Anton, self-hosted as `.woff2`
- **Hosting:** Vercel

## 🚀 Run Locally

No installation is needed.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Musamahlangu1977/mahlangu-portfolio.git
cd mahlangu-portfolio
```

Then open `index.html` in your browser.

## 🌐 Deployment

The site is deployed on **Vercel**. Because it is a static site, it needs no build command. Every push to `main` redeploys it automatically.
